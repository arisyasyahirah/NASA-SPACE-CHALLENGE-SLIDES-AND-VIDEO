# Crew Health Monitor

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** · Team Ad Astra Abyssosque
*Challenge: Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions*

A self-check and emergency guidance app for astronauts on deep space missions, where a delayed signal to Earth means no doctor on call. **Tap. Check. Act.**

## What it does

- **Tappable body map** with green, yellow and red status for each indicator
- **Camera heart-rate scan** with no extra hardware, processed on the device
- **7-day trends**, plain-language guidance with sources, and alerts for red readings
- **Quick Aid** step-by-step guides with video for CPR, choking and the EpiPen
- Works offline and on phone or desktop
